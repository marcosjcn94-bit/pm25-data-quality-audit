# Prespecified analysis protocol

Study four Los Angeles County monitors in calendar 2024 using EPA AirData daily summaries, daily_88101_2024.zip. Freeze this protocol before examining concentration results. Source metadata alone identified 1,424 records. Select state 06, county 037, parameter 88101, sample duration 1 HOUR, blank pollutant standard, event type None, units Micrograms/cubic meter (LC). Include the four site/POC pairs 1103/3, 1302/3, 4009/3 and 9035/1. Preserve leading zeros. Use local dates and reject duplicate monitor-days.

A valid day has a finite arithmetic mean and at least 18 hourly observations. Form all 48 monitor-months including missing months. Coverage is valid days divided by calendar days. Include monitors under three rules: at least one valid day, at least 75% coverage, or at least 90% coverage. Within each month compute the unweighted mean of eligible monitor means and the mean across all their valid monitor-days. Define the signed difference as equal-monitor minus equal-day concentration, in micrograms per cubic meter. For each rule report eligible monitor-months and monthly composition. Compare eligibility rules holding weighting fixed, and weights holding eligibility fixed. Empty sets have missing estimates.

Implement pandas and SQLite independently from extracted raw rows. Require exact key, count and missingness agreement, absolute error at most 1e-9 for concentrations. Validate with hand-computed cases for leap years, coverage boundaries, duplicate keys, missing months and unequal weights. Compare hashes of canonical result tables in a clean clone. Do not infer population exposure or regulatory compliance.
